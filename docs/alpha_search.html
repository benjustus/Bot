Is there any exploitable pre-PDUFA alpha vs XBI?

Not "is there a run-up" — that's just sector beta. The real question, chased by twelve independent research streams and put on trial against overfitting: does any conditioning rule beat the biotech index out-of-sample, after costs?

Verdict

No. Across 10,000+ tested subsets, combinations, clusters and hypotheses on real data, zero survive multiple-testing correction + out-of-sample replication + realistic costs.

Every rule distilled from 2025 either fades or flips negative on the 2026 & 2022–23 hold-outs. The one persistently-positive corner (illiquid micro-caps) is net-negative after real spreads. Five further streams (FDA designations, clinical, financials, short interest, options) are untestable on free data — open questions, not disproven.

E

Monte-Carlo: leverage-to-beta, or flat

100,000 roll-over paths from €1,500. End capital by sizing, vs. simply holding XBI over the same span.

Median / expected end capital. All-in also carries a 3.4% chance of losing >half (CVaR₅ ≈ €647).

Just holding XBI (+76.5%) beats the all-in strategy's median (+49%) with half the drawdown and no ruin tail.

All-in "works" only by levering full capital into a rising sector — that's beta, priced with a −57% tail. Prudent fixed 5% sizing is flat after costs: no growth once the beta leverage is removed. There is nothing left to compound.

G

Going deeper: every subset, combination and cluster

Then we searched harder — 10,054 feature combinations, 33 clusters, an expanded ML with new features (relative strength vs XBI, beta, ATR), and — newly unlocked via free openFDA data — Priority-vs-Standard review. The multiple-testing pile is exactly what the theory predicts.

2/3/4-way combinations. 1,202 look significant (≈503 expected by chance) — 0 pass BH-FDR, 0 survive the hold-out.

The single best combination — {low RS, low momentum, high beta, XBI<200d} — scores +10.3%, t=4.87, 95% hit in-sample. But it needs "XBI below its 200-day MA", so it selects zero events in the 2026 tape and is negative on the 2 regime-holdout events it has. With 10,000 tries you are guaranteed such a mirage.
  • Clusters: silhouette 0.22 (no clean groups); 0 of 33 survive both hold-outs.
  • Relative strength vs XBI: no alpha (flips −1.8% OOS).
  • Priority Review (now testable): Priority −1.3% ≤ Standard +2.3% — the opposite of the naive bet, and Standard flips −11.6% in the bear regime.
  • ML v2: OOS-AUC 0.57, selected net excess +0.14% (≈0); trees below chance.
H

Where alpha could still hide (honestly open)

The remaining untestable streams need paid point-in-time data — verified directly: Yahoo returns only a current short-interest snapshot and current options chains, no history; clinical-trial quality and company financials have no free structured historical feed. (FDA review priority was newly unlocked via openFDA and tested above — no alpha.) The likeliest remaining hiding places are short-interest / squeeze dynamics, options signals (IV rank, gamma) and insider / secondary-offering timing — needing FINRA, ORATS/CBOE, S&P Capital IQ. Until then their existence is neither confirmed nor refuted; the burden of proof stays with the strategy.

Method. Discovery on 2025 (in-sample, n=257 tradeable); validation on 2026 (n=122) and 2022–23 (regime hold-out) — nothing tuned on the hold-outs. Metric throughout: XBI-excess. Prices Yahoo v8 (split/div-adjusted); PDUFA dates & outcomes pdufa.bio; approvals cohort drugs.com. All features strictly point-in-time (no look-ahead); label-shuffle confirms no leakage. Only real historical data — nothing estimated or invented.
